## 👋 About Me

🎓 Mathematics graduate from **Andalas University**, focused on the intersection of **applied mathematics, data analytics, and machine learning**.

My undergraduate thesis built a **hybrid epidemiological–machine learning model**, combining the SIR-UV compartmental framework with Support Vector Regression to predict dengue fever transmission from climate data — developed during a research internship at **BRIN RI** (National Research and Innovation Agency).

Outside research, I led **HIMATIKA** (Mathematics & Data Science Student Association) as President, coordinating 58 members across 7 divisions.

📍 Padang, West Sumatera, Indonesia &nbsp;·&nbsp; 📫 dimasdamarn@gmail.com

## 🎯 What I Do

- 📊 **Data Analysis** — turning raw datasets into dashboards and decisions (Power BI, Excel, SQL, Tableau)
- 🧮 **Mathematical Modeling** — building differential-equation and compartmental models for real-world dynamics
- 🤖 **Machine Learning** — applying regression and predictive models to strengthen mathematical frameworks
- 🗣️ **Data Storytelling** — communicating technical findings to non-technical audiences (proven through 3+ years as a teaching/lab assistant)
